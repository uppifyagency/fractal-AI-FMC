How to read the cones:
Each cone = scatter of N walker positions at the planning horizon, in that car's frame. A wide cone = the car is uncertain. A narrow cone = the car has converged on a strategy. Two cones converging at the same point = the cars are about to interact (and the FMC virtual reward will steer them apart via the distance term).
