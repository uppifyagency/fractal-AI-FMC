🛣️ FMC highway — autonomous lane-changing in real time

Three-lane highway, cars enter from the left at varied target speeds. Each car runs its own FMC swarm of N walkers, M ticks ahead, choosing among {accel, brake hard/soft, lane up, lane down, cruise}. Reward: +speed (more is better), penalties for guardrail (off-road) and collision. Watch the cones ahead of each car — slow cars get small cones, fast cars about to overtake have wide cones spanning multiple lanes as the swarm evaluates "stay vs change lane".

Safety ON = reactive TTC layer brakes hard if collision in 1.6 ticks (the practical setup). Safety OFF = pure FMC, the swarm decides everything. With Safety OFF you'll see aggressive overtakes and frame-perfect lane changes — and more crashes. This is "the intelligent self-suicide" pattern: the swarm finds paths that maximize future speed while threading impossibly close gaps. Near-misses (cars within 1.5× collision distance) are tracked separately to show this risk-taking behavior.
What you should see: fast cars accumulate behind slow cars, then their cones widen vertically (the swarm explores "lane change" options), and they overtake. A well-tuned FMC keeps collisions ≈ 0 and avg speed close to the target mix.
